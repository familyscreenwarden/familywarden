Screen-time rules for families

Screen time with rules you set, on their PC and phone.

Family Screen Warden lets you set daily time limits, bedtime, and which apps and websites are allowed on your kids' Windows PCs and Android phones. You run it from a web console that works on your own phone.

Made for parents and guardians. One account covers the whole family, with unlimited kids and devices.

What it does

Time only counts while an app or website is open. The home screen and family calls are free.

Time limits and bedtime

Decide how much screen time each kid gets, and when screens go dark for the night.

  • Daily limits, set separately for school days and weekends
  • Bedtime, when screens switch off for the night
  • Pause all screens with one button

Apps and websites

Choose what each kid can open, and how long for.

  • Per-app limits
  • “Free” apps that never count against time
  • Browsing limited to websites you allow
  • SafeSearch and YouTube Restricted Mode

Kids can ask, you answer from anywhere

When time runs out or an app is not allowed, your kid can send a request instead of arguing with the screen.

  • Kids can ask for more time or a new app
  • You say yes or no from the console, wherever you are

The phone stays reachable

A locked phone is still a phone. Your kid can always reach the people who matter.

  • Family contacts can always be called or texted
  • Emergency calls always work
  • Family calls never use up screen time

Reports

See how the week went without standing over a shoulder.

  • Weekly reports of screen time
  • A log of blocked websites

What it does not do

It is a set of limits, not a way to read your kid's private life.

  • Does not read messages
  • Does not track location
  • No ads, and no selling of data

How it works

Three steps. You do not need a card to start.

  1. Sign up with your email

    Enter your email and open the link we send you. There is no password to remember.

  2. Add a kid

    Type a first name or nickname, then set their daily limits and bedtime. Add as many kids as you have.

  3. Install on the PC or set up the phone

    Install the app on a Windows PC, or set up an Android phone with a QR code. The rules you chose start applying on that device.

Questions parents ask

Is it really free?

Yes, during early access. There is nothing to pay and we never ask for a card.

If we introduce paid plans later, we will email you before anything changes. You will not be charged without choosing a plan yourself.

Can my kid uninstall it?

It is built to make that hard, but no parental control is impossible to get around, and we will not promise otherwise.

On Android, the phone is set up in what Android calls device-owner mode, which is why setup needs a factory reset. On a Windows PC, give your kid a standard Windows account, not an administrator one.

The console shows each device as in use, locked or off. A device that has not checked in for a few minutes shows as off, so you can notice if one goes quiet.

Does it read messages or track location?

No. It does not read messages, and it does not track location.

It sees which apps and websites are open and for how long, so it can count time and apply your rules.

What data is kept?

Only what the service needs to work:

  • Your email address
  • Each kid's first name or nickname
  • Device names and models
  • App and website names, with minutes used
  • Addresses of websites that were blocked
  • Family contact names and phone numbers that you enter
  • Requests your kids send you

Kids do not create accounts. There are no ads and data is not sold. If your account ends, its data is deleted 60 days later. The privacy draft has the details.

Which devices does it work on?

Kids' Windows PCs and Android phones. It does not work on iPhone, iPad, Mac or Chromebook. Android setup needs a factory reset.
