Privacy policy

Last updated: [DATE]. This page explains what Family Screen Warden collects, what it does not collect, and what choices you have.

Who we are

Family Screen Warden is run by [COMPANY LEGAL NAME], [COMPANY ADDRESS]. In this policy, “we” means that company. You can reach us at [YOUR SUPPORT EMAIL].

Who the service is for

Family Screen Warden is for parents and legal guardians. The parent creates the account, sets the rules and sees the reports.

Kids do not create accounts and do not give us an email address or password. A kid's device applies the rules the parent has set, and the kid can send the parent a request, for example for more time.

What we collect

We collect only what the service needs to work:

  • Parent email address. Used to sign you in with an email link and to send you messages about your account.
  • Kid first name or nickname. You type this in. A nickname is fine; we do not ask for a full name or date of birth.
  • Device names and models. For example “Max's PC”, with the device's model and software versions, so you can tell devices apart.
  • App and website names, with minutes used. So we can count screen time, apply limits and show you reports.
  • Blocked website addresses. When a kid's device blocks a website, the address of that site and the time are saved so you can see them in the blocked-site log.
  • Family contact names and phone numbers that the parent enters. So the phone can let those people be called or texted.
  • Requests kids send. For example a request for more time or for a new app or website, and your answer.
  • Texts and family chat from a kid's phone. Text messages and picture messages sent and received in the phone's Texts app, and messages in the family chat, are copied to the service so a parent can read them in the console. Messages include the names and numbers of the people the kid texts.
  • Photos, videos and movies from a kid's phone. Pictures and videos taken with the phone's camera are copied to the service so a parent can see them. A video shared with family by link can be opened by anyone who has that link until the link ends.
  • Location of a kid's phone. When a parent has location sharing on for a kid, the phone reports where it is about every 5 minutes (more often while a parent has the map open, or for 30 minutes after the kid asks for help), together with its battery level, so a parent can see it on a map. A Check in sends one position. A parent can turn sharing off for each kid. Only Android phones set up with the QR code share location.

We also keep the settings you choose (limits, bedtime, allowed apps and websites), your time zone, and whether you are on a trial or a paid plan.

What we do not collect

  • No messages from other apps. We do not read or store email, or messages inside chat apps a parent chooses to allow. Only the phone's own Texts app and the family chat are copied, as described above.
  • No location from Windows PCs. Only a kid's Android phone reports where it is.
  • No browsing history beyond blocked attempts and time spent on allowed websites.
  • No ads. We do not show ads and we do not use your data or your kid's data for advertising.
  • No selling data. We do not sell personal information to anyone.

How we use the information

  • To apply the rules you set on your kids' devices.
  • To show you the dashboard, reports and requests in the console.
  • To sign you in and to send you messages about your account, such as sign-in links.
  • To take payment if you choose a paid plan.
  • To keep the service working and secure.

Children's privacy

The service handles a small amount of information about children, because that is how screen-time rules work. We have written this section around the ideas in the US Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA).

The parent gives consent

Information about a kid is only collected after a parent or guardian creates an account, adds the kid and sets up the kid's device. By doing that, you give your consent to the collection described on this page. Kids cannot sign up by themselves.

The parent is in control

You decide what name to give your kid in the console, which devices are set up, and which contacts are added. You can change the rules or remove a device at any time.

You can review your kid's information

You can see the information we hold about your kid in the console: their name, devices, apps and websites with time used, blocked websites, contacts and requests. If you want a copy, ask us.

You can delete it and stop further collection

You can delete a kid from the console, which removes that kid's information. You can also ask us to delete your kid's information, or your whole account, and to stop collecting more. If you ask us to stop collecting, the service can no longer work for that kid.

We keep it to what is needed

We do not ask a kid for more information than the service needs, and we do not use kids' information for ads.

How to ask

Write to [YOUR SUPPORT EMAIL] from the email address on your account. We may ask a question or two to check that you are the parent on the account before we act.

How long we keep it

We keep your information while your account is open. When an account ends, its data is deleted 60 days later.

Some things are deleted sooner. Positions of a kid's phone are kept for 30 days unless the parent chooses 1 or 90 days; check-ins for 90 days. Photos, videos and movies from a kid's phone are kept for 90 days. A parent can delete a kid's location history, photos and videos at any time, and removing a kid or a phone deletes them.

If you delete a kid, a device or a contact in the console, it is removed from your account then.

Who helps us run the service

We use other companies to run parts of the service. They handle information only to do that job for us. By category:

  • Hosting. The servers and database where the service runs and where account data is stored. [HOSTING PROVIDER]
  • Email delivery. Sends sign-in links and account messages to the parent's email address. [EMAIL DELIVERY PROVIDER]
  • Payments. Takes card payments for paid plans. Your card details go to the payments company; we do not store your card number. [PAYMENTS PROVIDER]

We may also share information if the law requires it.

This website and cookies

This sales website does not use advertising or tracking cookies and has no analytics scripts. It loads its fonts from Google Fonts, so your browser contacts Google to fetch them.

The console uses one cookie to keep you signed in.

Security

Sign-in is by email link, so there is no password to steal. No system is perfectly secure, and we cannot promise that information will never be accessed without permission.
