Setup guides

Set up a kid's device

Plain steps for a Windows PC and for an Android phone. Start with the three steps everyone does, then follow the guide for your device.

  • Before you start
  • Windows PC
  • Android phone

The kid apps are not released yet. The Windows installer and the Android setup QR code are marked “Coming soon” below. You can sign up and set your rules in the console today. The steps on this page describe how setup will work; small details may change when the apps are released.

Before you start

Do these once, on your own phone or computer.

  1. Sign in to the console

    Go to app.familyscreenwarden.com and enter your email. We send you a link; open it to sign in. There is no password.

  2. Add your kid

    In the console, add a kid with a first name or nickname. Set their daily time limits for school days and weekends, and their bedtime.

  3. Get a pairing code

    In the console, open Devices and choose “Add a device”. Pick the kid and the kind of device. The console shows a pairing code of 8 letters and numbers. It works once and runs out after 30 minutes, so get it when you have the device in front of you.

Windows PC

For a PC your kid uses. You need to be able to install programs on it.

Windows installer: coming soonThe download is not available yet.

  1. Use a standard account for your kid

    On the PC, make sure your kid signs in to Windows with a standard account, not an administrator account. Keep the administrator password to yourself.

  2. Download the installer

    When the Windows installer is released, the download will appear on this page.

    Coming soon. There is nothing to download yet.

  3. Install the app

    Run the installer on the kid's PC and follow the steps on screen.

  4. Enter the pairing code

    When the app asks, type the 8-character pairing code from the console. This links the PC to your kid.

  5. Check the console

    Open Devices in the console. The PC should be listed under your kid's name. From now on, the limits, bedtime, and app and website rules you set apply on this PC.

Android phone

For a phone your kid uses. Setup uses a QR code and needs a factory reset.

QR code not released yet
Android setup QR code: coming soonThe QR code is not available yet.

A factory reset erases the phone. Photos, apps, messages and accounts on the phone are removed. Back up anything you want to keep before you start. If the phone is new and still on its welcome screen, you can skip the reset.

  1. Back up the phone

    Save photos and anything else you want to keep somewhere safe. After the next step they will be gone from the phone.

  2. Factory reset the phone

    In the phone's Settings, look for the reset options and choose to erase all data (factory reset). The name and place of this setting differs between phone makers. When it finishes, the phone restarts on its welcome screen. Stop there; do not go through the normal setup.

  3. Scan the setup QR code

    The welcome screen is where the phone can be set up from a QR code. When the setup QR code is released, this page will show it and the exact steps to scan it.

    Coming soon. The QR code is not available yet.

  4. Connect to Wi-Fi and wait

    The phone asks for a Wi-Fi network, then installs Family Screen Warden by itself. This can take a few minutes.

  5. Enter the pairing code

    When the app asks, type the 8-character pairing code from the console. This links the phone to your kid.

  6. Add family contacts

    In the console, add the family contacts your kid can always call or text, even when the phone is locked. Emergency calls always work.

Why the reset? Android only lets an app manage the whole phone (Android calls this device-owner mode) when it is put in place during first-time setup.

Not supported

Family Screen Warden does not work on iPhone, iPad, Mac or Chromebook. There are no setup steps for those devices.
